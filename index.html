Results & Discussion

The six tasks provided different perspectives on application popularity, user engagement, category performance, time-based growth and revenue.

Application Popularity

Installation counts were used throughout the project as a major indicator of application reach and category performance.

User Engagement

Ratings and review counts provided complementary measures of user satisfaction and interaction.

Revenue Analysis

The final task showed that strict filters can leave very few Paid applications, making the revenue comparison highly selective.
